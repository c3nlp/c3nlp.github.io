Helena Gómez Adorno

Professor
National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM)

Helena Gómez Adorno is a professor at the Research Institute in Applied Mathematics and Systems (IIMAS), UNAM. She earned her Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Center for Computing Research in Mexico. Her research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and information retrieval, with work spanning clinical NLP, semantic similarity, authorship analysis, and opinion mining on social media. She is a member of the Mexican National System of Researchers (SNII) of the Mexican Research Department. Dr. Gómez Adorno has published extensively in conferences and journals in NLP, has mentored numerous students, and has served as program chair, area chair, and reviewer for various national and international NLP conferences. She is an active board member of the ACL.

Title: Beyond Spanish: Cultural Grounding for Latin American NLP

Abstract: Large language models can work in many languages, but that does not mean they really understand the cultures behind those languages. This talk, will discuss why NLP needs to move beyond simply supporting Spanish and pay closer attention to the cultural and social diversity of Latin America. We will focus on how local knowledge, community-based data, retrieval methods, and better evaluation can help us understand where current models fail and how we can make them more useful for specific communities.
